❓Section 3: Questions to Ask Apoorva

Listed in recommended order (first to last)

Question 1: Strategic Technical Direction (Principal-Level Focus)

"The job description highlights developing core networking and routing components. As a Principal Engineer focused on the Data Plane, where is the most significant architectural trade-off happening right now? Specifically, how is the team balancing the conflicting demands of maintaining line-rate performance and ultra-low latency—which is Puli's team's charter—with the increasing complexity of L7 policy enforcement and the integration of AI/ML models for advanced security analysis? What is the biggest performance challenge we need to solve in the next 18 months?"

Why this works:

Uses correct jargon ("line-rate," "architectural trade-off," "L7," "AI/ML") and shows Principal-level thinking about system boundaries and performance bottlenecks. Demonstrates you understand the core tension in modern firewall design.

Question 2: Leveraging Past Architecture (User-ID/Geneve)

"I worked closely on the User-ID over NAT feature and recall the architectural discussions about leveraging the underlying Geneve-based tunneling mechanism as a foundational platform for future context-sharing features. Given the current push for platform consolidation and deeper L7 visibility: Is the team actively building new features that leverage or extend that Geneve architecture? I'm curious if that strategic vision materialized, as it seemed like a powerful foundation for reducing redundant signaling paths."

Why this works:

Demonstrates specific technical knowledge (Geneve protocol), shows you think about architectural reuse and efficiency, and connects your past work to future roadmap. Focuses on positive future vision rather than past problems.

Question 3: Onboarding and Immediate Impact

"As a Principal Engineer, my goal is to deliver immediate architectural impact. What would success look like in the first six months, and which specific, high-priority project—perhaps in the area of multi-core scaling or advanced packet processing—would I be expected to take ownership of and drive immediately? Essentially, what is the most pressing technical gap that needs to be filled on Day 1?"

Why this works:

Demonstrates commitment to immediate ownership and reinforces your "Day 1 Readiness" claim. Shows you want the most difficult, high-impact work, which is attractive to any hiring manager.

Question 4: Team Collaboration & Cross-Functional Work (NEW)

"The Data Plane team obviously has critical dependencies with other groups—L7 teams, Product Management, Cloud infrastructure teams. How does the Principal Engineer role facilitate collaboration across these boundaries? Specifically, are there regular architectural forums or design review processes where the Data Plane team presents performance constraints or influences roadmap prioritization for features that impact core throughput?"

Why this works:

Shows you understand Principal Engineers must work across organizational boundaries. Subtly assesses whether the Data Plane org has influence over other teams (good sign) or is constantly reacting to demands (red flag). Also demonstrates maturity about cross-functional leadership.

Question 5: Technical Mentorship and Team Growth (NEW)

"One aspect of Principal-level roles I find rewarding is mentoring senior engineers and helping elevate the team's technical capabilities. What opportunities exist within the Data Plane team for technical mentorship, knowledge sharing, or defining engineering best practices? Are there initiatives like tech talks, design review boards, or pair programming that the team uses to raise the collective technical bar?"

Why this works:

Shows you think beyond individual contribution to team/organizational impact. Demonstrates leadership mindset. Also gives insight into team culture and whether they value knowledge sharing vs. siloed work. This is particularly valuable given your past experience with dysfunctional team dynamics.
